# Key Insights & Recommendations

## Findings
- The dataset contains **12,719 interaction events**, **5,000 sessions**, and **1,872 users**.
- The most viewed page type is **home**.
- The most common entry page is **home**.
- The most common exit page is **product_page**.
- **Google** has the largest acquisition volume at **1,280 sessions**.
- **Google** has the highest referral-source conversion rate at **21.64%**.
- **Desktop** has the highest device conversion rate at **20.35%**.
- **Desktop** has the highest device bounce rate at **20.71%**.
- Overall conversion rate is **20.20%**.
- Cart abandonment is **36.84%**, while checkout abandonment is **10.06%**.

## Recommendations
1. Prioritize UX improvements on the most common exit pages, especially where users are expected to continue toward purchase.
2. Compare acquisition sources using both traffic volume and conversion quality rather than traffic alone.
3. Investigate device-specific usability issues for the highest-bounce device segment.
4. Simplify the cart and checkout journey and make costs, delivery information and calls-to-action clear.
5. Use page-level engagement and exit behavior to prioritize content and navigation improvements.
6. Re-run the analysis periodically after website changes to evaluate whether conversion and abandonment metrics improve.
